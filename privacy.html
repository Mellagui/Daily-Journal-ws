Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our utmost priority. Daily Journal is built from the ground up on an offline-first, local-storage architecture.

Summary: We do not collect, store, transmit, or monetize any of your personal data, journal entries, browsing history, or analytics. Everything stays strictly on your local computer.

1. Information Collection & Use

Daily Journal does not collect any personal data.

  • No User Accounts: You do not need to register, provide an email address, or sign in.
  • No Telemetry or Tracking: We do not use Google Analytics, cookies, pixels, or third-party trackers.
  • No Cloud Transmissions: Your journal entries are never sent across the internet to any external server.

2. Local Storage (`chrome.storage.local`)

All notes, daily habit streak counters, and theme preferences are saved locally on your computer inside Chrome's secure sandboxed storage (chrome.storage.local).

3. Permissions Requested

Daily Journal requests only the single minimal permission required to function:

  • storage: Used solely to save and retrieve your journal entries on your local hard drive.

4. Data Ownership & Exporting

You have 100% ownership over your journal data. You can export your notes at any time to standard Markdown (.md) files or clear your data with a single click in the extension.

5. Contact & Support

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, feel free to contact us via our Chrome Web Store support channel or GitHub repository.
