Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Daily Journal.

Where is my journal data stored? +

All your journal entries, streak milestones, and preferences are stored locally in your browser's secure sandboxed storage (chrome.storage.local). Nothing is ever sent to any external server.

Can I export my journal entries? +

Yes! Click on the Habit Streak popover in the extension header to export any year's entries or your complete archive as a beautifully formatted Markdown (.md) file directly to your downloads folder.

Is the extension completely free? +

Yes, Daily Journal is 100% free with no ads, no subscriptions, and no paywalled features.

Does it work without an internet connection? +

Absolutely. Because all logic and storage run locally inside your browser package, you can write, reflect, and browse your history anywhere, anytime—even offline in airplane mode.
